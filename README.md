## Scientific scope
The project reconstructs the documented standard workflow and evaluates a researcher-reported modified workflow used under laboratory reagent/material constraints.

The evidence supports feasibility of the adapted workflow. It does **not** establish superiority, quantitative equivalence, or causal attribution to any individual modification.

## Reported modifications
- **M1:** modification of the relevant initial reaction/processing conditions under reagent constraints.
- **M2:** dilution.
- **M3:** a small modification in the transformation stage.

Exact undocumented parameters are intentionally not reconstructed.

## Claim boundary
The defensible conclusion is that the adapted workflow was experimentally successful under the reported constraints. This project does not claim that the modified workflow was better than the standard workflow.
