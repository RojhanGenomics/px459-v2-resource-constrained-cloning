# Final Publication-Readiness Audit

**Project:** PX459-v2 gRNA Cloning Under Resource Constraints  
**Version:** 1.0.1  
**DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.22086082  
**Audit date:** 2026-08-25

## Overall assessment

**Status: Research-artifact ready / publication-ready as a retrospective, DOI-archived research output.**

This repository is suitable for public scholarly use, citation, portfolio presentation, and research discussion. It should **not** be represented as a controlled comparative protocol-validation study or as evidence of superiority over the standard workflow.

## Evidence limitations that remain intentionally visible

- Exact parameters for undocumented modifications are unavailable and are not reconstructed.
- Quantitative efficiency and replicate-level comparison are not established.
- The available evidence supports feasibility, not superiority or statistical equivalence.
- Individual causal attribution among multiple modifications is not established.

These are **scientific limitations, not publication-readiness defects**, because they are explicitly disclosed and reflected in the claim boundary.

## Final editorial recommendation

The repository can be presented as a **DOI-archived retrospective research artifact / case study**. For any future journal manuscript, the experimental evidence would need to be expanded into an appropriate comparative design with quantitative replicates and statistical analysis; that is a separate research phase and is not required to make the present repository honest, citable, and researcher-ready.

## Final verdict

**READY — with the stated evidence boundary.**
