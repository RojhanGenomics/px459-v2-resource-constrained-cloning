# Final Project Report

## PX459-v2 gRNA Cloning Under Resource Constraints

### Author
Mahdieh Rojhannezhad, Ph.D.  
ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0009-2427-718X

## 1. Objective
Document a resource-constrained adaptation of the PX459-v2 gRNA cloning workflow and define the strongest claims supported by the surviving evidence.

## 2. Standard workflow
The documented workflow comprises gRNA/oligo preparation, PX459-v2 preparation and BbsI processing, insert/vector joining, bacterial transformation, colony-check PCR, culture of positive colonies, plasmid extraction, and sequence confirmation.

## 3. Reported modifications
Only three researcher-reported modifications are retained:

- **M1:** modification of the relevant initial reaction/processing conditions under reagent constraints.
- **M2:** dilution.
- **M3:** a small modification in the transformation stage.

The surviving record does not establish exact numerical parameters or reagent identities for these modifications, so they are not reconstructed here.

## 4. Evidence
The standard workflow is documented by the thesis and published article. Experimental images provide observed evidence of colonies and colony-PCR bands. The modified condition is represented by the single-plate colony image and the corresponding modified colony-PCR image; the four-plate colony images represent the standard condition. A duplicated standard colony-PCR image was previously supplied in error and is not treated as independent modified-condition evidence.

Sequence confirmation for modified clones is reported, but underlying sequencing traces are not included in the available evidence package.

## 5. Claim calibration
The evidence supports a feasibility claim: the adapted workflow was successfully carried out under the reported resource constraints and produced experimental evidence consistent with successful cloning.

It does **not** support claims that the modified workflow was better, statistically more efficient, quantitatively equivalent, universally applicable, or that any individual modification caused the success.

## 6. Limitations
Missing or incomplete information includes exact M1-M3 parameters, quantitative colony counts, transformation efficiency, PCR-positive fractions, independent replicate data, and raw sequencing traces for the modified condition.

## 7. Conclusion
This project is best described as a **resource-constrained, experimentally successful adaptation of the documented PX459-v2 gRNA-cloning workflow**, not as an improved or superior protocol.
