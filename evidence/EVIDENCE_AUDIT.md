# Evidence Audit

## Evidence classes
- **Documented:** Standard workflow steps in the thesis/article.
- **Observed:** Colony growth and colony-PCR bands in supplied images.
- **Reported:** Resource constraint, M1-M3, and reported sequencing confirmation.
- **Unknown:** Exact modification parameters, quantitative efficiencies, replicate-level comparison, and modified-condition sequencing traces.

## Image mapping
- **Standard:** four-plate colony image set; standard colony-PCR image.
- **Modified:** single-plate colony image; modified colony-PCR image.
- A previously supplied duplicate of the standard colony-PCR image is not independent modified evidence.

## Claim boundary
The evidence supports feasibility of the modified workflow. It does not support superiority, statistical equivalence, or individual causal attribution.
