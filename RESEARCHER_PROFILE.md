# Researcher Profile

## Mahdieh Rojhannezhad, Ph.D.

**Genetics Researcher | Functional & Regulatory Genomics | Cancer Genomics | CRISPR/Cas9 | Computational Genomics**

Mahdieh Rojhannezhad is a Ph.D. geneticist and genomics researcher whose work spans experimental molecular genetics, cancer functional genomics, genome editing, molecular diagnostics, and computational analysis of genomic data.

Her doctoral research focused on the functional and bioinformatic investigation of a HER2-associated regulatory enhancer in breast cancer using CRISPR/Cas9, integrating wet-lab genome editing with molecular and computational interpretation. Her broader research experience includes clinical molecular and cytogenetic diagnostics, protocol optimization, NGS workflow development, and reproducible computational analysis.

### Research experience supporting this project

This project reflects the experimental side of a broader research profile that combines hands-on molecular genetics with evidence-based protocol evaluation. Rojhannezhad has professional experience in molecular and cytogenetic diagnostic laboratories and doctoral-level experience in CRISPR/Cas9-mediated functional genomics.

The present repository is intentionally framed as a **retrospective research artifact**. Its conclusions are limited to the evidence available for the documented resource-constrained adaptation and should not be interpreted as a controlled comparative validation study.
