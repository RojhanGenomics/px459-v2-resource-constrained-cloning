# Portfolio Case Study

## Resource-Constrained PX459-v2 gRNA Cloning

**Role:** Researcher / protocol optimization  
**Author:** Mahdieh Rojhannezhad, Ph.D.

### Problem
The documented cloning workflow depended on laboratory resources that were not always available. The project asked whether the work could still be completed using a practical adapted workflow.

### Approach
The overall PX459-v2 cloning architecture was retained. Three researcher-reported adaptations were used: M1, M2 (dilution), and M3. The surviving evidence does not provide enough detail to reconstruct undocumented parameters.

### Evidence
Standard-condition colony images comprise the four-plate set. The modified-condition colony evidence is the single-plate image. Colony-PCR evidence exists for both conditions; a duplicated standard image is excluded from independent modified-condition evidence.

### Outcome
The adapted workflow produced colonies and colony-PCR evidence consistent with successful cloning, with sequence-confirmed clones reported.

### Scientific claim
The project demonstrates **practical feasibility under resource constraints**. It does not claim superiority or prove causality for any individual modification.

### Skills demonstrated
Molecular cloning, CRISPR/gRNA workflow handling, colony screening, evidence reconstruction, protocol optimization, scientific claim calibration, and reproducibility-oriented documentation.
